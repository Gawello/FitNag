FitNag - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Overview

FitNag is a fitness tracking application that respects your privacy. We believe your workout data belongs to you and only you.

Data Collection

FitNag does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All data you enter into the app — including your name, workout history, body measurements, exercise preferences, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device in a local database. No data is ever sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device using an SQLite database. This data remains on your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted.

Exercise Images

The app loads exercise demonstration images from a publicly available open-source repository (free-exercise-db). These image requests are standard HTTPS requests and do not include any personal or identifying information.

Third-Party Services

FitNag does not integrate any analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other third-party services that could collect your data.

Permissions

The app requests only the following device permission:

  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback during exercise tempo guidance.

Children's Privacy

FitNag is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
